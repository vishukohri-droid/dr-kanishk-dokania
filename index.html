Meet Dr. Kanishk Dokania

MBBS, MS (General Surgery), MCh (Urology)

Dr. Kanishk Dokania is a highly reputed Urologist and Renal Transplant Surgeon practicing primarily at Charak Hospital, Dubagga, Lucknow. Known for his empathetic approach and precise diagnoses, he has successfully handled numerous complex urological cases across India.

He specializes in advanced laser surgeries for kidney stones (RIRS/PCNL), minimally invasive prostate therapies, and complete kidney transplant clinical frameworks, offering quick patient rehabilitation timelines.

  • MBBS - West Bengal Univ. of Health Sciences (2010)
